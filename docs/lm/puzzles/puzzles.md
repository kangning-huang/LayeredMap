# NimbleMap Pro puzzles

Eight original "where is it?" puzzles for testing NimbleMap Pro end to end and for the release
videos. Each is solved by a plan of NimbleMap actions; actions marked **new** are the proposals in
[mechanics.md](mechanics.md). Every answer was checked by the reference solver
`tools/lm/puzzles/solve.py` on the open datasets described in
`/Volumes/LaCie/data/layeredmap-puzzles/README.md`, which also printed the candidate counts and robustness
ranges below. The machine-readable version is [puzzles.json](puzzles.json); answer geometries
are in [answers.geojson](answers.geojson). This page is generated by `tools/lm/puzzles/render.py`.

## P1 · Grandma's Flower Postcard

**Difficulty:** easy. **Setting:** China. **Answer type:** city.

Xiaomei lives in Harbin, where January snow piles up to the windowsills. This winter her grandma moved south to be near her aunt, and the first postcard has just arrived: a photo of a market stall heaped with flowers, and Grandma in a thin cotton jacket. "It's January and I haven't worn my down coat once," she wrote. "The city is big and busy, but it sits so high up in the mountains that I was out of breath on the stairs for my first week." There is no postmark, because the corner got wet. Xiaomei wants to put a pin in the map above her bed. Can you help her find Grandma's new home?

**Clues**

1. It is a Chinese city of more than two million people.
2. It is at least 1,500 metres above sea level.
3. Its January average temperature is at least 5 °C.

**What the user types into Pro**

- English: "Which city in China has more than two million people, is at least 1,500 metres above sea level and has a January average of 5 °C or warmer?"
- 中文：“中国哪座城市人口超过两百万、海拔至少1500米、一月平均气温不低于5°C？”

**Datasets:** Natural Earth 1:10m populated places (v5.1.2); WorldClim 2.1 elevation, 10 arc-minutes; WorldClim 2.1 January mean temperature, 10 arc-minutes.

**Expected plan**

| Step | Action | On | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `filter` | Populated places | ADM0NAME is China, POP_MAX above 2,000,000 |
| 2 | `add_raster_values` | Populated places | elevation |
| 3 | `add_raster_values` | Populated places | January mean temperature |
| 4 | `filter` | Populated places | elevation at least 1500 and January at least 5 |

**Answer:** Kunming (昆明), China: 25.043° N, 102.697° E. Yunnan's capital, long called the Spring City; its flower market is one of Asia's largest.

## P2 · Aurora Week

**Difficulty:** easy. **Setting:** North America. **Answer type:** city.

The astronomy club at a school in Singapore has never seen snow, let alone the northern lights. Their teacher, Mr Rahman, has found a sister school in North America willing to host them for one week in January. The club voted on one rule: it has to be properly cold, the kind of cold where your breath freezes on your scarf, because the clearest, darkest nights come with it. Mr Rahman also wants a real city with a science museum and a planetarium, not a tiny town. The club has the list of possible host cities. Which one wins the vote?

**Clues**

1. The city is in North America.
2. At least 500,000 people live there.
3. Its January average temperature is −15 °C or colder.

**What the user types into Pro**

## P3 · Grandpa's Squid Boat

**Difficulty:** medium. **Setting:** South Korea (East Asia). **Answer type:** island.

Ji-woo's grandfather fished for squid for forty years. On summer nights the boats hung rows of bright lamps over the water, and from far away the sea looked like a city floating in the dark. He grew up on a small island that belongs to South Korea, and he says it is so far out that on a clear day you still could not see any other land, only water in every direction. When Ji-woo asked how big it was, he laughed: "Big enough for a school, a harbour and a mountain; small enough to walk around in a few days." Ji-woo wants to surprise him with a trip back. Which island is it?

**Clues**

1. The island is part of South Korea.
2. Its area is between 50 and 100 km².
3. The nearest other land, island or mainland, is at least 75 km away.

**What the user types into Pro**

- English: "Which island of South Korea is between 50 and 100 square kilometres and at least 75 km from any other land?"
- 中文：“韩国有哪个岛面积在50到100平方公里之间，并且离其他任何陆地都至少75公里？”

**Datasets:** Natural Earth 1:10m land (v5.1.1) and minor islands (v4.1.0); Natural Earth 1:10m admin-0 countries (v5.1.1).

**Expected plan**

| Step | Action | On | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `split_parts` **new** (A7) | Land and Minor islands | one feature per island |
| 2 | `join_by_location` **new** (A6) | Islands | add country ADMIN from Countries |

| 4 | `filter` | Islands | country is South Korea, area between 50 and 100 |
| 5 | `nearest_distance` **new** (A3) | Islands | to the nearest other island or mainland (same-layer variant) |
| 6 | `filter` | Islands | distance at least 75 km |

**Answer:** Ulleungdo (郁陵岛), South Korea: 37.497° N, 130.861° E. A volcanic island in the East Sea famous for squid fishing; the coordinates are a point on the island.

Robustness (each threshold moved alone):

- `area_min` = 50 km²: same answer from 10 km² to 70 km² or beyond

## P4 · The Tea Caravan Notebook

**Difficulty:** medium (six clues, but four are thresholds set in one find_where call). **Setting:** China. **Answer type:** area.

Clearing out her family's old house, Li Na found a cloth-bound notebook that belonged to her great-great-grandfather, who led mule caravans carrying tea. Most pages are lists of loads and prices, but one page describes where the best leaves grew: green hills, never lowland and never high peaks, warm all year, soaked by summer rain, with winters so mild the tea bushes never froze. He wrote that the tea country was one great stretch of land that took his caravan weeks to cross, all inside China, and that every load started from its market town. Li Na wants to find the tea hills, and the town, on a modern map.

**Clues**

1. Elevation between 1,000 and 2,000 metres.
2. Annual average temperature at least 17 °C.
3. Annual rainfall at least 1,300 mm.
4. January average temperature at least 10 °C.
5. Inside China.
6. One connected stretch of land of at least 10,000 km².

**What the user types into Pro**

- English: "Show me the areas in China between 1,000 and 2,000 metres where the annual average temperature is at least 17 °C, annual rain is at least 1,300 mm and January averages 10 °C or more. Which of those areas is bigger than 10,000 square kilometres, and which town is in it?"
- 中文：“在中国找出海拔1000到2000米、年平均气温不低于17°C、年降水量至少1300毫米、一月平均气温不低于10°C的地区。其中哪一片面积超过一万平方公里？里面是哪座城镇？”

**Datasets:** WorldClim 2.1 elevation, 10 arc-minutes; WorldClim 2.1 BIO1 annual mean temperature, 10 arc-minutes; WorldClim 2.1 BIO12 annual precipitation, 10 arc-minutes; WorldClim 2.1 January mean temperature, 10 arc-minutes; Natural Earth 1:10m admin-0 countries (v5.1.1); Natural Earth 1:10m populated places (v5.1.2).

| 2 | `keep_inside` **new** (A2) | Areas found | inside the Countries feature China |
| 3 | `add_measurements` **new** (A4) | Areas inside China | area_km2 (recomputed after the clip) |
| 4 | `filter` | Areas inside China | area_km2 at least 10,000 |
| 5 | `whats_inside` | the remaining area | Populated places inside it |

**Answer:** Tea hills of south-west Yunnan around Pu'er (Simao) (滇西南普洱（思茅）一带的茶山), China: 23.037° N, 100.078° E. The coordinates are the area's centroid. Pu'er gave its name to the tea and was a starting point of the old tea-and-horse caravan roads.
Town: Simao (思茅, now the centre of Pu'er city), 22.781° N, 100.978° E.

## P8 · The Red Crab Crossing

**Difficulty:** hard. **Setting:** Indian Ocean (Australia). **Answer type:** island.

A class in Chengdu received a letter from Ranger Aisha, who looks after a national park on a faraway island. Once a year, when the first heavy rains come, millions of bright red land crabs leave the forest and march to the sea to lay their eggs. Roads close, bridges are built just for crabs, and children help count them. Aisha forgot to say where she lives, but left a riddle on the back of the photo: the island lies between the tropics, is a bit smaller than a big city, and is far from every other speck of land. It is very rainy. And if you ignore the signs, its longitude is ten times its latitude. Where should the class post their reply?

**Clues**

1. The island is between the Tropic of Cancer and the Tropic of Capricorn.
