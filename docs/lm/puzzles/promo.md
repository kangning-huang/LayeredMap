# Promo storyboards: three puzzles solved by Pro

Storyboards for 30–45 second release clips in which LayeredMap Pro solves a puzzle from
[puzzles.md](puzzles.md). The three chosen are the most visual: P4 (areas growing and shrinking
over Yunnan as conditions are added), P7 (three borders meeting by a river) and P8 (a hunt across
every ocean that ends on one island).

Rules for all three, from the spec (Product tiers; Phase 7 › The Ask box; Preview; Result card):

- The question goes into the Ask box at the bottom of the map ("Ask about your layers"), typed or
  dictated with the microphone button. There is no chat transcript: the answer appears as the
  plan's **preview banner** ("Preview — not saved", with Keep and Discard), striped preview
  geometry with "Preview" in the legend, and a **result card** with title, answer, context lines,
  buttons (Show on map · View table · Change question · Export) and "How this was calculated".
- Every step shown on screen is a real action in the plan. Actions marked **new** in puzzles.md
  must exist before filming; until then the clips can only be mocked, and must not be released as
  real footage.
- Captions are the on-screen text; there is no narration over the map other than optional music.
- End card credits: "Data: WorldClim 2.1 (CC BY 4.0), Natural Earth, GEM Global Active Faults
  (CC BY-SA 4.0)" as each clip uses them. No Smithsonian volcano data appears in any clip.
- Layers are opened beforehand from the puzzle data pack, so the clip starts with them on the
  shelf; the basemap is the app's default light style.

## Clip 1 · P4 "The Tea Caravan Notebook" (40 s, in Chinese, with English subtitles)

The most visual: a sequence of map states where coloured areas appear, shrink and finally leave
one region with a town in it.

| Time | Screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Close-up of an old notebook page beside a laptop (live action or illustration), then cut to LayeredMap showing south China; the layer shelf lists Elevation, Annual mean temperature, Annual precipitation, January mean temperature, Countries, Populated places. | 曾曾祖父的茶马笔记 · Great-great-grandfather's tea notebook |
| 4–10 s | The cursor clicks the microphone in the Ask box; the Chinese request from P4 appears word by word as it is dictated: "在中国找出海拔1000到2000米、年平均气温不低于17°C……其中哪一片面积超过一万平方公里？里面是哪座城镇？" | 用一句话提问 · Ask in one sentence |
| 10–14 s | A plan summary above the Ask box lists the steps (Find areas where conditions hold → Keep the part inside China → Add area → Filter → What's inside?). The spec does not yet define a plan summary; drop this shot if the Ask box does not show one. | Pro plans the steps with certified actions |
| 14–24 s | Preview banner "Preview — not saved". Striped areas build up as each condition joins: first all the 1,000–2,000 m hills (a large orange band across south-west China and beyond), then warmer, then wetter, then frost-free in January. A small counter in the card summary updates: 1,075 → 573 → 355 → 356 areas. The view eases from all of Asia to Yunnan. | 海拔 · 年均温 · 降水 · 一月气温 |
| 24–30 s | Everything outside China fades away (15 areas left), then the small ones fade; one large area remains over south-west Yunnan, outlined. | 只剩一片：五万七千平方公里 · One stretch left: 57,516 km² |
| 30–36 s | Keep is clicked. The result card slides in: title "茶山在哪里" (Where the tea hills are), big number 57,516 km², context line "Inside: Simao (思茅), 162,725 people". The map pin on Simao pulses; its label shows 思茅 · 普洱. | 答案：普洱（思茅）的茶山 · Answer: the tea hills of Pu'er |
| 36–40 s | "How this was calculated" opens for two seconds, showing the recipe lines (inputs, thresholds, method "cells on the WorldClim 10′ grid, 8-connected"). End card with the LayeredMap Pro logo and data credits. | 每一步都可以复查 · Every step is checkable |

## Clip 2 · P7 "Three Flags and a Thousand Waterfalls" (35 s, English)

Visual hook: three countries' colours meeting where two rivers join.

| Time | Screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|

| 4–10 s | The P7 English request is typed into the Ask box at a natural speed. | Ask in plain words |
| 10–18 s | Preview banner. Every South American city appears as a dot (1,042), then dots fade as each clue applies: rain (343), within 10 km of a major river (48; the rivers briefly glow), cool July (7). Seven dots remain, labelled. | 1,042 → 343 → 48 → 7 |

| 31–35 s | Result card: title "City with three countries nearby", answer "Foz do Iguaçu, Brazil", context "Ciudad del Este, Paraguay, passed every clue but the last". Buttons visible. End card with credits. | Answer: Foz do Iguaçu |

## Clip 3 · P8 "The Red Crab Crossing" (42 s, English, Chinese subtitles)

Visual hook: a whole-globe search where thousands of islands blink out until one is left.

| Time | Screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | A child's letter with a red crab drawing (illustration), then the map zoomed out to the whole world with all islands outlined in teal. | A letter from a ranger on a faraway island |
| 5–11 s | The P8 request is typed into the Ask box. | Ask in plain words |
| 11–15 s | Plan summary, if the Ask box shows one (Split into islands → Add area and coordinates → Filter → Distance to the nearest other land → Summarize rain → Calculate a ratio → Filter). | Seven certified steps |
| 15–23 s | Preview banner. The tropics band is shaded; islands outside it fade (3,876 left), then islands of the wrong size fade (346). The counter ticks down in the card summary. | Tropical · 50–150 km² |

## Production notes

- Record at the real speed of the app with the evaluation layers, then trim waits; never speed up
  or fake a preview. Counts on screen must match `puzzles.json` (the solver's numbers); if the
  certified actions give different counts, update the captions, not the data.
- Chinese captions for clip 1 should be checked by a native speaker; the request text is in
  `puzzles.json`.
- Stock photos and footage (waterfall, crabs) need their own licences; LayeredMap screens and the
  data credits above cover the maps.
