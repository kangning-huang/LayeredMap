# Naming

The app was called **LayeredMap** until 2026-10-19. The working name is now **NimbleMap**, and **千层图** in Chinese
(owner's choices, 2026-10-19). A formal trademark check is still pending. This page keeps every
name considered, so the search can be picked up again without starting over.

Availability checks below are point-in-time: domains were checked through the registries' RDAP
services on 2026-10-19, GitHub and crates.io by URL, products by web search. **None of this is a
trademark clearance.** That needs a search of USPTO, EUIPO/TMview, WIPO and CNIPA in classes 9
(software), 42 (software services) and 41 (education), ideally by an attorney, plus an early
filing in China (first to file) for both the English and the Chinese name.

## Current decision

| | |
|---|---|
| English name | **NimbleMap** (Pro: NimbleMap Pro) |
| Why | Says what the app is next to QGIS: light and quick, not a heavy GIS. That contrast is also the search angle (people looking to open a file "without QGIS"). Contains "map", as the owner wanted. |
| Availability (2026-10-19) | nimblemap.com, .app and .io unregistered; GitHub `nimblemap` and crates.io `nimblemap` free. No "NimbleMap" product found. Nearest: Nimblehold, an unreleased tabletop-game map tool on Steam. |
| Known risks | Medium overall (vetting below). **nimblemaps.com is a live "Nimble Maps" web map site** (registered 2026-01). "Nimble" is crowded in software (159 live USPTO NIMBLE marks, about 40 owners in classes 9 and 42), so each one is weak and the name's strength rests on the combination. "Nimble" isn't CET-4/6 vocabulary, so a strong Chinese name matters. |
| Chinese name | **凌波微图** (owner's choice, 2026-10-19, replacing 千层图). A pun on 凌波微步, light-footed movement (Cao Zhi's 《洛神赋》, and Duan Yu's skill in Jin Yong's 《天龙八部》), so it carries "nimble" and "light" like the English name. The Chinese pages show both, **凌波微图 NimbleMap**, so the bilingual pun is visible: in the header, titles, link previews and footers, and as 凌波微图（NimbleMap） at first mention. Needs a CNIPA search for 凌波微图 and 凌波 (classes 9, 42); vetting below. 千层图 ("thousand-layer map") was the choice before it. |
| What was renamed | Everything users see: app and bundle names, menus, the website and help, exports, diagnostics, installers. See "Where the display name is set" and "What keeps the old name, and why" below. |

**No identical mark found.** The USPTO search backend returns no marks for NIMBLEMAP, NIMBLE*MAP* or NIMBLE MAP(S). Nothing called NimbleMap is in the Apple App Store (US and China), the Mac App Store, Google Play, the Microsoft Store, npm, PyPI, crates.io or Product Hunt.

|---|---|---|

| Nimble (Nimbleway) [Maps API](https://docs.nimbleway.com/nimble-sdk/web-api/vertical-endpoints/maps-api) | map-data scraping | Medium |
| NIMBLE marks with the nearest goods: Spark Wave (AI and data-science tools, 90798279), Nimble Robotics (AI software, 87842188), Nimble Elearning (educational software, 5619496), Nimble Hub (geospatial software, 90332782) | classes 9 and 42 | Low–medium |
| Nimble CRM, HPE Nimble Storage | software | Low |
| Enforcement found so far: one board opposition, Nimble Communications v. NYMBLE AGENCY ([TTAB 91270108](https://ttabvue.uspto.gov/ttabvue/ttabvue-91270108-OPP-4.pdf)) | | info |
| Student GitHub repos, the Nimble GM Maps tabletop module, Nimblehold | games and hobby | Low |

**Second round of Chinese names (owner preferred 轻图, 2026-10-19).** 轻图 itself is taken by photo-editing apps (same class 9). Alternatives checked on the China App Store: 敏图, 轻舆, 秒图, 瞄图, 巧图, 翩图 and 轻叠 had no app of that name. Ruled out: 闪图 (three apps, including CAD闪图), 飞图 (飞图影像), 速图, 快图 (快图浏览), 叠图 and 简图 (too generic).

**敏图 vetting (2026-10-19): high risk, not adopted.**
- 成都中科合迅科技 (Sinux) has marketed **合迅敏图 / 中科敏图 "QuickMap"**, a lightweight 2D/3D GIS platform, since 2021 ([listing](https://www.ruanfujia.com/software/642599/), updated 2024). Same name, same field. It may be dormant, but a filed mark would block us.
- "敏图转换器", a media converter (mintuware.com, registered 2022).
- Companies: 上海敏图科技, 深圳市敏图科技, 唐山敏图科技 (holds a software copyright) and 昆明敏图科技 (software and IT services).
- 闽图 is an exact homophone, used by Fujian Library's 掌上闽图 app.
- mintu.com, .cn and .com.cn are taken. mintu.app, mintu.io and 敏图.com look free.

**凌波微图 (owner's idea, 2026-10-19): adopted the same day.** A pun on 凌波微步, the light-footed
movement from Cao Zhi's 《洛神赋》 ("凌波微步，罗袜生尘"), which most readers know as Duan Yu's skill in
Jin Yong's 《天龙八部》. It carries "nimble" and "light" at once, so it matches NimbleMap better than
千层图 does. Checks:
- No exact-phrase use found on the web or on the China App Store (0 results). Searching 凌波 or
  凌波微步 there brings up Tencent's 天龙八部手游 and unrelated apps; none use 凌波微图.
- Its short form will be **微图**, which is weak: the China App Store's 微图 results are screenshot
  and marketing tools, and 微 reads as "tiny" or WeChat-ish as much as "light".
- The phrase itself comes from a public-domain poem, but the association is Jin Yong's. His
  publisher has enforced rights over his works before; a one-character pun on a skill name is far
  from those cases, but a lawyer should say so before launch.
- Four characters: fine as an app name and in the App Store; long in conversation.
- Needs a CNIPA search for 凌波微图 and 凌波 in classes 9 and 42.
- No trademark register could be queried: CNIPA, tmkoo, 天眼查 and 企查查 all blocked automated access.

**Runner-ups (quick checks):**
- 秒图: no. secondmap.cn is a "秒图" 3D map company, and there are photo apps.
- 瞄图: cleanest, with no company or app hits, but it means "glance or aim at a picture" and loses "nimble".
- 轻舆: no hits, but reads as classical (a light carriage) or as 舆论 (public opinion).

The suggested pairing is NimbleMap with 千层图, carrying "nimble" in a descriptor such as 千层图 · 轻巧的地图工具. Unrelated name pairs are common (TikTok/抖音, RED/小红书).

**Also found:** [GeoLibre Desktop](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/geolibre-desktop/id6796848769), a free Mac GIS viewer from Qiusheng Wu (opengeos, the RustGIS maintainer), released 2026-08. Not a name conflict, but a direct competitor.

After a rename, the approved UI snapshots that show the name in the toolbar must be re-approved
(`UPDATE_SNAPSHOTS=1 cargo test -p lm-ui screenshots`), and so must the import-notice snapshots
(`LM_UPDATE_SNAPSHOTS=1 cargo test -p lm-jobs snapshot`). Compare each new image with the old one
first: only the name may differ. Screenshots on the website (`web/lm/img/`) are retaken by hand.

## What keeps the old name, and why

These are identifiers, not display text. Changing them would break saved data, installed copies,
keys, permissions or upstream merges.

- **Crates, packages and the binary:** `lm-*`, the directory `apps/layeredmap`, the executable
  `layeredmap` (`layeredmap.exe`, `layeredmap-pro.exe`), the repository name.
- **Bundle identifiers and types:** `app.layeredmap.desktop`, `app.layeredmap.pro` and the
  UTType `app.layeredmap.map`, the MIME type `application/vnd.layeredmap.map+zip`. Changing a
  bundle id makes macOS treat the app as a new one (lost permissions, window state and file
  associations).
- **The `.lmap` extension and its format docs** (`docs/lm/format-v1.md`).
- **Windows identifiers:** the installers' AppIds, the ProgIDs `LayeredMap.*` and
  `LayeredMapPro.*`, the manifest's `assemblyIdentity`. The install folder does follow the name
  (`…\Programs\NimbleMap`); an upgrade over an existing install keeps its folder, as Inno Setup
  reuses the previous folder for the same AppId.
- **User folders** (`lm_core::platform`): `~/Library/Application Support/LayeredMap`,
  `~/Library/Logs/LayeredMap`, `~/Library/Caches/LayeredMap`, `%APPDATA%\LayeredMap`,
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\LayeredMap\…`, `~/LayeredMap/logs`, and the temporary folders
  `LayeredMap`, `LayeredMap-logs` and `LayeredMap packages`. **Moving these is a separate
  task:** it needs a one-time migration of autosaves, the recovery journal, the question log and
  the caches.
- **Keychain and environment:** the keychain item (service `openrouter`, account `layeredmap`),
  the scrambling pad for the test builds' built-in key (`LayeredMap Pro test build: …` in
  `lm-ask/src/openrouter.rs`, `bundle-macos.sh` and `build-installer.ps1`, which must agree), and
  every `LM_*` environment variable.
- **Other identifiers:** the PDF's fallback font name `LayeredMapFont`, the `layeredmap.app`
  domain in the assistant's `HTTP-Referer`, the CI workflow and job names (they are the status
  checks branch rules name), and a hash test vector in `lm-jobs/src/fingerprint.rs`.
- **Records:** `docs/lm/decisions.md`, the spec's phase records (Phases 0–6), `gates/`,
  `docs/lm/reviews/`, `docs/lm/netcdf-spike.md`, the benchmark sources and their converters
  (`tools/lm/benchmarks/`, `docs/lm/benchmarks/coverage.md`) and the evaluation results in
  `tools/lm/llm_eval/results/`.
- **Fixtures and archives:** `reference/`, `fixtures/`, `validation/`, `spikes/`, `design/`
  (including the logo files) and `web/lm/assets/logo.svg`.
- **Upstream RustGIS files:** the `LayeredMap:` notes in files that come from RustGIS
  (`Cargo.toml`, `.gitignore`, `xtask/src/main.rs`, `xtask/Cargo.toml`, `crates/assistant`,
  `crates/data`, `crates/map`, `crates/render`, `crates/tiles`), kept to make upstream merges
  simple.

## Why LayeredMap was dropped (2026-10-19)

- **layeredmaps.app** is a live, paid web app, "Layered Maps", from STRICS IT GmbH (Freistadt,
  Austria). It makes layered topographic maps for laser cutting, at €4.99, €14.99 and €49.99 a month. The domain was registered 2026-03-25.
  Different users (makers), but commercial map software in the same classes, one letter apart.
- **layeredmaps.com**: GoDaddy "launching soon" page; registered 2019 behind a privacy service.
- **layeredmap.com** (singular): registered 2025-08-23 at IONOS by an unknown owner, showing an empty
  German-language page.
- maker.studio sells a "Layered Map Maker".
- "Layered map" and "map layers" are everyday GIS terms, so the name is descriptive: weak as a
  trademark, and impossible to rank for in search.

## Before the name is final

1. A formal trademark search for NimbleMap, and for the chosen Chinese name.
2. Buy nimblemap.com, .app and .io (and .cn if it suits a China site).
3. Claim the GitHub org and social handles (X, Instagram, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, a WeChat official account).
4. Then rename the internal identifiers listed above, with a data-folder migration.
