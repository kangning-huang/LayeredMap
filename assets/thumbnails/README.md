# Basemap chooser thumbnails

Crops of NimbleMap's own map view over the Golden Gate (view -122.455, 37.795, zoom 12.5),
captured from the app window on 2026-10-17 with `--tour "basemap light|dark|satellite"`, cropped
to 3:2 and resized to 288 × 192 (3× the chooser's 96 × 64 tiles). The Offline thumbnail is drawn
in code.

| `satellite.png` | NASA Blue Marble Next Generation (NASA GIBS, zoom-4 tiles over the Mediterranean, Black Sea and Caspian), stitched and scaled to 288 × 192 on 2026-10-18 | NASA imagery, not subject to US copyright; credit NASA |

The credits match the ones the map shows when each basemap is on.
