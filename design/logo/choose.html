LayeredMap icon concepts

App icon directions for LayeredMap. The first round had six concepts; you picked Strata, and the five variations below build on it. Pick one by its name; it can still be refined after you choose.

Strata variations

You chose Strata. These five build on it; the designer recommends v2 Link, using v1's simplified artwork at 16 and 32 px.

Strata v1

Bold

Three bigger, plainer sheets with a wider gap. The 16 and 32 px sizes have hand-simplified artwork (16 px is three flat diamonds).

Strength
Clearest at every size.
Weakness
The plainest; loses the points and districts that give Strata its story.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
Strata v2

Link

Designer's pick

A line drops from one point on the top sheet through the district sheet to the same spot on the raster, with a marker on each layer: same place, different data.

Strength
The only variation that shows how the layers relate, not just that there are several.
Weakness
The drop-line only shows from 128 px up; small sizes would use v1's simplified artwork.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
Strata v3

Blue

The squircle in the app's accent blue, with a solid white top sheet and see-through white sheets beneath.

Strength
The strongest Dock presence of the five.
Weakness
Turns into a white blob on blue at 16 px; would need its own small-size artwork.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
Strata v4

Cutout

Only the raster is a full square. Above it float a road network and a single district shape, cut to the same footprint.

Strength
Most like real map data; less like a generic layers symbol.
Weakness
The road layer disappears below 128 px.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
Strata v5

Night

A dark navy icon with the sheets fanned like cards, so the raster glows from below.

Strength
Distinctive and calm; suits dark mode.
Weakness
Dark on dark in a dark Dock (a faint rim keeps the outline); fanned sheets weaken the "same footprint" idea.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px

First round

1

Strata

Your pick

Three map sheets stacked over one footprint: a temperature raster at the bottom, district boundaries in the middle, points on top. It is the app's own layer model, drawn as it is.

Strength
The clearest of the six at 16 and 32 px; uses the app's blue and the viridis ramp.
Weakness
Stacked sheets are a familiar "layers" symbol, so it is less distinctive as a brand.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
2

Overlay

A buffer circle and a district overlap. Only the area they share turns blue, with one point inside it: the spatial relationship itself, rather than the layers.

Strength
The most abstract and ownable mark; reads well small.
Weakness
Says "map" less directly; could pass for a generic Venn diagram.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
3

Fold

A folded paper map whose three panels are three layers (contours, districts, points), tied together by one route running across all of them.

Strength
Warm and instantly a map at large sizes.
Weakness
The busiest option: it turns to mush at 16 px, and folded maps are a common map-app image.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
4

Strata Pin

A location pin cut into three bands in the point, area and raster colours, standing on a quiet street grid: one place, many layers.

Strength
Strong silhouette; recognisable at every size.
Weakness
Pins are the most common map-app symbol, so it sits close to generic map icons.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
5

Swipe

One map split by a compare handle: the plain base map on the left, the same districts and river as a data layer on the right.

Strength
Tells the comparison story; distinctive colour block.
Weakness
At 16 px it becomes a two-tone tile; the handle reads as a code symbol to some people.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
6

Peel

A blue base-map sheet with its corner peeled back to reveal a raster layer underneath: what lies below the map you see.

Strength
The boldest silhouette and colour in the Dock.
Weakness
Page-curl icons are associated with documents and older UI styles.
Sizes 64 · 32 · 16 px
LayeredMap
All six are 1024 px macOS-style icons (rounded square body, flat colours, no text). The sources are in design/logo/ in the repository; a chosen icon gets hand-tuned 16 and 32 px versions before it ships.
