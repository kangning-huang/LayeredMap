What it opens

The files you actually download

Drag a file into NimbleMap and it lands in the right place, in the right projection, ready to explore.

  • .shp

    Shapefile

    Boundaries, roads, storm tracks. Keep the .dbf, .shx and .prj files beside it.

  • .zip

    Zipped Shapefile

    Open it as it is, no unzipping needed.

  • .gpkg

    GeoPackage

    One tidy file that can hold many layers.

  • .geojson.json

    GeoJSON

    The web’s map format, from open-data portals and APIs.

  • .csv.tsv.xlsx

    Tables

    CSV, TSV or Excel with latitude and longitude columns.

  • .kml.kmz

    KML and KMZ

    Placemarks, paths and areas from Google Earth.

  • .gpx

    GPX

    Runs, rides and hikes from your watch or phone.

  • .tif.tiff

    GeoTIFF

    Elevation, land cover, satellite and drone images.

  • .nc

    NetCDF

    Climate and weather grids, played with a time slider.

Double-click opens Shapefiles, GeoPackages and NetCDF files in NimbleMap. Other files open in other apps when double-clicked, so drag them into NimbleMap, or right-click › Open With › NimbleMap.

Once it’s open

Then explore

Zoom and pan

Scroll, pinch and drag, from the whole planet down to a single coast.

Search places or your data

Type a city to fly there, or a name to find it in your file: here, every storm called Haiyan.

Click anything

See what it is and how it compares. The 2011 Tohoku earthquake was stronger than 99% of 14,537.

Play time

Files with time steps get a slider. Press Play and watch a summer of heatwaves move.

Every number comes from all your data, not just what’s on screen, and every step can be undone.
