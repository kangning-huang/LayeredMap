A map app for the curious

Open a map file. See what it says.

NimbleMap is for people who love maps but have never used GIS. Double-click a file of places, tracks or climate data and it is on the map. Click anything and it tells you where it stands: the strongest storm, the largest walled city, the hottest week.

How it works

Layers, like tracing paper

A map in NimbleMap is a stack of layers on a background map. You don't set anything up first: there is no wizard, no project and no empty toolbox.

Open

Drag in a CSV with coordinates, a GeoJSON, a Shapefile, a GeoPackage, a GeoTIFF or a NetCDF climate file. If the app has to guess something, such as which columns hold the coordinates, it says so in one line and lets you change it.

Click

Select a city, a storm or a single cell of a climate grid. Before offering any tool, the card tells you where it stands: “highest of 4,999”, “lower than 80% of the others”.

Share

Colour by a value, filter to what matters, then export a finished image with a title, legend, scale bar and credits, sized for a slide, an A4 page or a square post.

Built-in examples

Things an everyday map app can't show you

Eight example sets come with the app, one click away under Examples. A few favourites:

Typhoons and hurricanes

4,999 storm tracks since 1980 in every ocean, from NOAA’s IBTrACS archive. Find Haiyan, colour them by wind and watch the typhoon belt light up.

IBTrACS v04r01 · 1980–2026

Walled cities of China, 1866

1,789 city walls of late imperial China, from the capital to county towns and Manchu garrisons, drawn over satellite imagery.

Xue et al. 2021 · CC BY 4.0

Heatwaves, summer 2023

Daily heatwave intensity worldwide from June to August 2023, a NetCDF climate file with a time slider. Press Play and watch the heat move.

Copernicus GDO (JRC) · CC BY 4.0

Also included: big earthquakes since 1900, volcanoes, airports, power plants, and world cities, countries and elevation.

Free and Pro

Free to look. Pro to ask.

The free app opens, overlays, styles and shares map data. NimbleMap Pro adds an assistant you ask about your layers in plain words.

Free

Free download

  • Opens CSV, Excel, GeoJSON, Shapefile, GeoPackage, KML, GPX, GeoTIFF and NetCDF
  • Click anything for its facts and where it stands
  • Colour by a value, filter, compare, label
  • Joins a spreadsheet to places by a shared column
  • Time slider for climate grids
  • Light, dark and satellite basemaps; works offline
  • Handles China's map coordinates (GCJ-02, BD-09)
  • Exports map images and data

Pro PRO

Everything in Free, plus:

  • The Ask box: ask about your layers in plain English or Chinese, typed or spoken
  • Answers come as a preview on the map and a step-by-step recipe you can check, never a guess
  • Built-in world layers: countries, provinces, cities, rivers, lakes and elevation, ready for questions like “Which country has the most power plants?”
  • Analysis buttons on cards, such as “Show an area within a distance”
  • Your data stays on your Mac: only layer names, fields and extents are sent, never the values
