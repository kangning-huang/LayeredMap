Glossary

Map words, in plain English

NimbleMap uses everyday words on its buttons, but map data comes with its own vocabulary. Here is what each word means and where you meet it in the app. Search in English or Chinese.

Ask box (Pro) 提问框（Pro） #

In NimbleMap Pro, a box at the bottom of the map where you ask about your layers in plain words, typed or spoken, in English or Chinese. The assistant picks the app's own checked steps; the numbers are always computed on your computer, and only descriptions of your layers (names, fields, extent), never their values, are sent.

In the appThe field Ask about your layers at the bottom of the map, with a microphone for dictation.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsoassistant · AI · natural language · question · chat
Assumption 假设 #

A choice the app made for you when opening a file, such as which columns hold coordinates or what a column means. Instead of asking questions, NimbleMap applies its best guess, says it in one line, and lets you change it in one click.

In the appThe Assumptions part of a layer's card, each with Change.
Alsoguess · interpretation · detected · 推断 · 识别
Attribute 属性 #

A fact stored with each feature, such as a storm's name, its season or its top wind speed. All the values of one attribute make a field, which is one column of the table.

In the appThe card's facts list, the Values part of a layer's Overview, and the columns of View data. Details › Fields lists each field and how the app reads it (Value, Category, Name, Date…).
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsofield · column · property · value · 字段
Attribute table 属性表 #

The spreadsheet view of a layer: one row per feature, one column per attribute. Selecting a row selects the feature on the map, and the other way round.

In the appView data on a card, or the layer's right-click menu. The drawer opens at the bottom of the map; click a column heading to sort.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsotable · spreadsheet · view data · 表格 · 数据表
Basemap 底图 #

The background map under your data: streets, coastlines, place names, or satellite imagery. It is there for orientation; it isn't one of your layers and isn't part of your data.

In the appThe Basemap button at the bottom right of the map: Auto (follows light or dark mode), Light, Dark, Satellite (NASA Blue Marble) and Offline, an outline map that needs no internet. Background strength fades it so your data stands out.
See it in a workflowWalled cities of China, 1866 →
Alsobackground · base map · satellite · tiles · 卫星图
Buffer 缓冲区 #

The area within a set distance of something: everywhere within 100 km of Hong Kong, or within 50 km of each storm track. GIS software calls it a buffer; NimbleMap calls it “Show an area within a distance”.

In the appThe card's Show an area within a distance of each button, free for everyone. In NimbleMap Pro you can also ask in the Ask box, for example “Show everything within 100 km of Hong Kong”.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsowithin a distance · radius · circle around · 距离范围 · 周边
Clip 裁剪 #

Keeping only the part of a layer that lies inside an area, like cutting a map with scissors along a border. The source file is never changed; the result is a new layer.

In the appThe card's Keep the part of [layer] inside [other] button, free for everyone; the result's recipe names the step the same way. In NimbleMap Pro you can also ask, for example “Keep the part of the storm tracks inside the area I drew”.
Alsocut · keep inside · crop · intersect · 相交
Coordinate system (CRS) 坐标系（CRS） #

The rules that turn a pair of numbers into a place on Earth. The same numbers mean different places under different systems, so a file has to say which one it uses. The most common is WGS 84, the system GPS uses.

In the appDetails › Coordinate system. When the app has to guess, the layer's card says so in one line under Assumptions, with a Change link.
AlsoCRS · coordinate reference system · EPSG · datum · spatial reference
Extent 范围（外包框） #

The rectangle that just contains all of a layer's data, from its westernmost to easternmost and southernmost to northernmost point.

In the appDetails › Extent; Zoom to layer and View › Zoom to All Data (⌘0) fly to it.
Alsobounds · bounding box · bbox · 边界 · 外接矩形
Feature 要素 #

One thing in a vector layer: one city, one storm, one walled town. On the map it is a shape; in the table it is one row.

In the appClick a feature on the map to select it. The card on the right shows its name, its facts, and up to three insight lines such as “highest of 4,999”.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsoobject · record · row · shape · 对象
Filter 筛选 #

Showing only the features that meet a condition, such as “strength is Category 5”. The others are hidden, not deleted, and the count of what is shown stays on screen.

In the appThe Filter button on a card writes a sentence you finish, like Show storms where strength is any of Category 5, then Apply. The layer then reads Filtered: with a Clear link.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsoquery · select by attribute · where · show only · 过滤
GCJ-02 and BD-09 GCJ-02 与 BD-09（火星坐标、百度坐标） #

The coordinate systems used by maps in mainland China. GCJ-02 (Amap/Gaode, Tencent) shifts positions by a few hundred metres from GPS; BD-09 (Baidu) shifts them a little more. Points copied from these apps land a few streets off on any other map unless they are converted.

In the appWhen points look like Amap or Baidu coordinates, the app asks once: Amap/Tencent coordinates (GCJ-02): convert or GPS coordinates (WGS 84): keep where they are, and offers Compare on the map first.
AlsoMars coordinates · Amap · Gaode · Tencent · Baidu
Geodesic 测地线（大地线） #

Measured along the curved surface of the Earth, the way a plane actually flies, instead of along a straight line on a flat map. On a flat map the shortest route from Shanghai to New York looks curved; geodesically, it is the straight one. NimbleMap measures distances and areas this way, so a 100 km circle is 100 km everywhere, near the equator or near the poles.

In the appBehind every distance and area the app shows: the scale bar, area facts such as area · 23.4 km², and analysis distances such as “within 100 km”.
Alsogreat circle · true distance · ellipsoid · 大圆 · 球面距离
GeoJSON, GeoPackage, Shapefile GeoJSON、GeoPackage、Shapefile #

Common files for vector data. GeoJSON is a single text file; GeoPackage (.gpkg) is a single database file that can hold several layers; a Shapefile is a set of files with the same name (.shp, .dbf, .shx, .prj) that belong together.

In the appToolbar Open (⌘O), or drag files into NimbleMap (Drop to add to the map). Details › Format names the format.
Also.geojson · .gpkg · .shp · file formats · KML
GeoTIFF #

An image file that also knows where on Earth it sits. Elevation models and satellite images often come as GeoTIFFs.

In the appThe World basics example's elevation layer is a GeoTIFF.
Also.tif · .tiff · DEM · elevation · satellite image
Insight line 对比信息 #

A sentence the app writes when you select something, telling you where it stands among the rest, such as “highest of 4,999” or “lower than 80% of …”. It comes before any tool, so you learn something just by clicking.

In the appThe top of the card after you click a feature or a raster cell. Compare with other [layer] shows it as a ranked chart.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsorank · compare · median · where it stands · 排名
Join 连接（表连接） #

Attaching a table of facts to places by a column they share, such as a spreadsheet of GDP by country name added to a map of countries. NimbleMap finds the shared column itself and says how many places matched.

In the appOpen a table (a CSV or Excel file without coordinates) while a map layer is open. The table appears indented under the layer, for example joined · 172 of 177 matched. Its menu has Show joined fields and Unjoin. A join needs a column whose values are unique in the layer (each country once); a column that repeats, like a type, can't be the key.
Alsotable join · attribute join · merge · match · 关联
Latitude and longitude 纬度和经度 #

A place's address on the globe. Latitude runs from −90° at the South Pole to 90° at the North Pole; longitude from −180° to 180° east or west of Greenwich. Shanghai is about 31.2° N, 121.5° E.

In the appA CSV with latitude and longitude columns (any common spelling: lat, lng, 纬度…) opens straight onto the map. Exported images can carry Lat/lon labels and Grid lines.
Alsolat · lon · lng · coordinates · degrees
Layer 图层 #

One set of things drawn on the map, such as every city, every storm track or a temperature grid. Layers stack like sheets of tracing paper: the one at the top of the list is drawn on top. Opening a file adds a layer; removing a layer never deletes your file.

In the appThe Layers panel on the left (toolbar button Layers, ⌘L). Each row has an eye to show or hide it; drag rows to reorder; right-click for Style…, Zoom to layer, Rename, Remove from map.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsolayers · shelf · overlay · 叠加
Legend 图例 #

The key that says what each colour or size means. When colours show a value, the legend shows the range; when they show categories, it lists each one.

In the appThe Legend section of a layer's Overview, and in exported map images.
See it in a workflowWalled cities of China, 1866 →
Alsokey · colour key · color key · 说明
Map image (export) 导出地图图片 #

A finished picture of your map with a title, legend, scale bar and credits, drawn at full size for a slide, a report page or a social post.

In the appToolbar Share › Map image… (⌘E). Sizes: Slide 16:9, Report A4, Square. Options: Title, Caption, Legend, Scale bar, North arrow, Lat/lon labels.
See it in a workflowThe 2023 heatwave over time →
Alsoexport · share · PNG · save image · print
NetCDF #

A file format scientists use for climate and ocean data: grids that change over time and often hold several variables (temperature, rainfall, wind). NimbleMap opens .nc, .nc4 and .cdf files like any raster, with a time slider and a variable picker.

In the appThe example Heatwaves, summer 2023. Under the layer's name in the Layers panel, the Variable shown pop-up picks the variable; a Level shown pop-up appears for data with depths or heights.
See it in a workflowThe 2023 heatwave over time →
Also.nc · nc4 · climate data · ERA5 · CMIP6
No data 无数据（NoData） #

A cell or field with no value, which is different from zero. In the heatwave example, days and places without a heatwave are no data, so they are left clear rather than painted as “0”.

In the appClicking such a place shows No value or No data. Summaries say how many were left out.
See it in a workflowThe 2023 heatwave over time →
AlsoNoData · missing value · fill value · null · blank
Projection 投影 #

The way the round Earth is flattened onto a flat map. Every flat map stretches something: on the usual web map (Web Mercator) Greenland looks as big as Africa, though Africa is about 14 times larger. NimbleMap draws in Web Mercator, like most map apps, but measures distances and areas on the real globe.

In the appYou don't need to choose one. A layer's Details tab shows the file's own Coordinate system; the app reprojects it for display and leaves the file untouched.
Alsomap projection · Web Mercator · Mercator · 墨卡托 · 地图投影
Raster 栅格 #

Data stored as a grid of cells, like the pixels of a photo, where every cell holds a number: an elevation, a temperature, a heatwave intensity. Zoom in far enough and you see the squares.

In the appClick a raster and the card shows that cell's value and where it stands (for example, how it compares with the rest of the grid). The Style tab sets its Palette and Range.
See it in a workflowThe 2023 heatwave over time →
Alsogrid · image · pixels · cells · GeoTIFF
Recipe 步骤说明（配方） #

The plain-words record of how a result was made: which layers went in, which step ran, over what scope, and what was left out. You can copy it as text and check it.

In the appEvery analysis result, from a card button or (in NimbleMap Pro) the Ask box, comes as a result card with its steps listed, a preview on the map, and Keep or Discard.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsoresult card · method · provenance · how it was made · 方法
Resolution (cell size) 分辨率（像元大小） #

How big each cell of a raster is on the ground. The heatwave and elevation examples use cells of 0.5° (about 55 km at the equator), so each value is an average over a big square, not a single spot.

In the appDetails for a raster layer.
Alsocell size · pixel size · 0.5 degree · grid spacing · 像元
Scale and zoom level 比例尺与缩放级别 #

How much of the Earth fits on the screen. Zoom level 0 shows the whole world in one tile; each step in doubles the detail. The scale bar shows what a distance on screen is on the ground.

In the appThe scale bar at the bottom left of the map; Zoom in and Zoom out at the bottom right (⌘+ and ⌘−). View › Metric Units or Imperial Units.
Alsozoom · scale bar · resolution · 缩放 · 比例
Style (symbology) 样式（符号化） #

How a layer looks: one colour, or colours that follow a value (dark red for strong storms) or a category (one colour per type of town). Colouring areas by a value is what cartographers call a choropleth map.

In the appThe Style tab: Color, Palette, Size, Labels, Opacity, Outline. The card's Color by a value button is the quick way in.
See it in a workflowWalled cities of China, 1866 →
Alsosymbology · symbol · colour by · color by · choropleth
Time step 时间步 #

One moment in data that changes over time: one day, one month or one year of a grid. The map shows one step at a time. The colour range stays the same for every step, so a change of colour is a real change of value.

In the appThe time slider at the bottom of the map: Previous step (←), Play, Next step (→), the date (such as “16 Jul 2023”) and the step count. The ← and → keys step too.
See it in a workflowThe 2023 heatwave over time →
Alsotime slider · frame · date · animation · play
Variable 变量 #

One kind of measurement in a gridded file, such as 2 m air temperature or heatwave intensity, with its unit. A NetCDF file can hold many; the map shows one at a time.

In the appThe Variable shown pop-up under the layer's name, for example “2 m temperature (°C)”.
See it in a workflowThe 2023 heatwave over time →
Alsoband · parameter · measure · 波段 · 要素变量
Vector data 矢量数据 #

Data made of shapes: points (a volcano), lines (a storm track) and areas (a city wall, a country). Each shape is one feature and carries its own facts. Vector data stays crisp at any zoom.

In the appA layer's subtitle says what it holds, for example 4,999 lines or 1,251 points. GeoJSON, GeoPackage, Shapefile, KML and CSV files with coordinates open as vector layers.
See it in a workflowTyphoons: the strongest storms →
Alsovector · points lines areas · shapes · 矢量
WGS 84 #

The worldwide coordinate system behind GPS: latitude and longitude in degrees. If a spreadsheet has latitude and longitude columns, they are almost always WGS 84.

In the appCards say Located using longitude and latitude as GPS coordinates (WGS 84), with Change.
AlsoGPS coordinates · EPSG:4326 · latitude longitude · GPS 坐标
Work offline 离线工作 #

NimbleMap reads your files on your own computer; nothing you open is uploaded. Online services are used only for the basemap, place search and update checks, and one switch turns all of them off.

In the appView › Work Offline. The basemap then switches to the offline outline map (Working offline: online backgrounds are off).
Alsooffline · no internet · network · privacy · 断网
