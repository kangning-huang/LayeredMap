The window in one minute

  • Toolbar (top): the search box Search places or your data, then Open (⌘O), Layers (⌘L), Undo (⌘Z) and Share (⌘E).
  • Layers panel (left): everything you have opened. The eye shows or hides a layer; drag to reorder; right-click for more.
  • Card (right): appears when you click something. It shows the facts, where the thing stands among the rest, and up to three suggested buttons. A layer’s card has Overview, Style and Details tabs.
  • Map controls (bottom right): Zoom in, Zoom out and the basemap button. The scale bar is at the bottom left.
  • Undo everything: every change, from a colour to a filter to removing a layer, is one step of Undo. Your files are never changed.

Opening your own files

Drag files into NimbleMap or use Open. NimbleMap reads CSV and Excel (with latitude and longitude columns), GeoJSON, Shapefile, GeoPackage, KML/KMZ, GPX, GeoTIFF and NetCDF. If the app had to assume something, such as which columns are coordinates, the card says so in one line with Change beside it. Points copied from Amap, Tencent or Baidu maps can be moved onto GPS coordinates; see GCJ-02.

Updating NimbleMap

NimbleMap checks for a new version when it opens, and when you choose NimbleMap › Check for Updates…. When there is one, a small notice appears at the bottom right of the map. Click Update Now: NimbleMap downloads the new version, checks its digital signature and checksum, replaces itself and reopens. If you have unsaved changes, it asks first. You don’t need to visit the website.

The maps update with the app. The example maps and the built-in world layers ship inside NimbleMap, so they are updated with it. The online basemap is always current.

If NimbleMap can’t replace itself, for example when it runs straight from the disk image or your account can’t change the Applications folder, it opens the new version in Finder: drag NimbleMap to Applications. Versions 0.7.1 and earlier have no Update Now: download the new version once from the download page.

Privacy. The check reads one small file from nimblemap.com, or its copy on GitCode, and sends nothing about you: no identifiers, only the app’s version number. With View › Work Offline on, NimbleMap doesn’t check.

Feedback

Send feedback: Help › Send Feedback… in the app, or GitHub Discussions.

Privacy

Feedback from the app. When you choose Help › Send Feedback…, NimbleMap sends what you write to our feedback service, which posts it publicly on GitHub Discussions (github.com/kangning-huang/NimbleMap/discussions), where anyone can read and reply. The post contains your message, your title if you gave one, the kind of feedback and the interface language. It includes your app and macOS version numbers only if you leave Include app version and macOS version ticked. It never contains your email address, your name, your account, your maps or your files. You don’t need a GitHub account.

If you give an email address, it is stored privately on our server next to the link to your post, only so we can reply to you. It is never shown publicly. We delete it when the conversation is over, and after a year at most; write to us to have it deleted sooner.

If you are signed in, the post says only that it comes from a signed-in user, and our server stores your account’s ID privately next to the link to your post, so we can follow up. It is never shown publicly, and we delete it when the conversation is over, and after a year at most.

To prevent spam, the app sends a random install number made on your computer, and our server sees your network address. Both are stored only as one-way keyed hashes for counting, and deleted after a week.
